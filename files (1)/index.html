About Me

I build things for the web & beyond

Hey! I'm Jisu — a full-stack developer and product designer based in Seoul. I work at the intersection of engineering and design, believing that great products are born when both disciplines speak the same language.

My focus is on creating clean, performant, and accessible experiences. I care deeply about the details: micro-interactions, typography, and the invisible decisions that make software feel right.
